## The problem

Finding something to watch means jumping between search, ratings, cast pages and a watchlist that lives somewhere else.

## What I built

A personal movie and TV discovery site with TMDB data:

- **Trending, popular and top-rated** movies and series, plus actor and director filmographies.
- **Discovery filters** for release year, minimum rating, language and genre, and instant search with keyboard suggestions (press `/`).
- **A watchlist and collections** saved in the browser, with one-click JSON backup and restore to move them between devices.
- **Playback through third-party embedded streams** with resume and next-episode. No video is stored or hosted by the site.

## How it works

The Next.js App Router app talks to TMDB only through a server-side `/api/tmdb` proxy, so the API key never reaches the browser. Library state uses React's `useSyncExternalStore` over `localStorage`, with no database.
