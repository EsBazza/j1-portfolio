## The problem

Finding something to watch means jumping between search, ratings, cast pages and a watchlist that lives somewhere else, usually behind an account.

## What I built

A personal, dark cinema-style discovery site built on TMDB data:

- **Catalogs for movies, series, anime and K-drama**, a Top 10 for today, trending and popular rows, and a "Surprise me" button.
- **Discovery filters** for release year, minimum rating, language and genre, plus instant multi-search across movies, series and people with keyboard suggestions (press `/`).
- **Rich details pages** with seasons and episodes, cast, and actor and director filmographies.
- **A library with no account and no database:** watchlist, collections, watch history and resume points live in the browser, with one-click JSON backup and restore to move them between devices.
- **A custom player** built on ArtPlayer.js and hls.js, with a subtitle sync slider, subtitle styling, local `.srt` and `.vtt` uploads, theater mode and keyboard shortcuts. Titles play through third-party embedded streams; the site does not store or host any video.

## How it works

The Next.js App Router app never exposes the TMDB key: every request goes through a server-side `/api/tmdb` proxy that injects the token and sets edge caching (a day for details, an hour for trending and discovery). Library state uses React's `useSyncExternalStore` over `localStorage`, and playback progress is saved every few seconds so titles resume where you left off.
