## The problem

Sometimes a line from a film says it better. Reel To You turns a movie quote into a message left for someone, that anyone can find by their name.

## What I built

A mobile-first time capsule with no accounts:

1. Search for a movie with TMDB.
2. Pick a curated quote, or write your own.
3. Add a short dedication and the recipient's name.
4. Submit, and get a direct link to share.

Anyone can type a name and read every message left for it, newest first.

## How it works

Entries live in Supabase Postgres, where the public can only read visible entries and every write goes through a server route. Upstash Redis rate-limits submissions, and names are normalised so searches match however they were typed.
