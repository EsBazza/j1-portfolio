## The problem

The IBM Bob Hackathon's challenge was to turn an idea into impact faster. Quick photo edits shouldn't need a heavy desktop editor.

## What I built

A browser photo editor with AI built in:

- **One-click AI background removal.**
- **Smart crop suggestions** from subject detection.
- **Color palette extraction** that turns a photo into design-ready colors.
- **Smart text fitting**, mouse-wheel zoom, aspect-ratio lock, undo and redo, and export of either the raw photo or the full canvas.

## How it works

The editor is a React and TypeScript app built with Vite. Fabric.js drives the canvas, TensorFlow.js with COCO-SSD runs subject detection in the browser, and Colorthief extracts palettes. The project was planned and coded with the assistance of IBM Bob.
