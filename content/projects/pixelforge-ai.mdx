## The problem

The IBM Bob Hackathon's challenge was to turn an idea into impact faster. Quick photo edits like removing a background, finding a crop or pulling a palette shouldn't need a heavy desktop editor or an upload to someone else's server.

## What I built

PixelForge AI is a local-first photo editor that runs entirely in the browser:

- **One-click AI background removal**, processed on the device.
- **Smart crop suggestions** from subject detection.
- **Palette extraction** that turns any photo into a five-color HEX palette you can copy in one click.
- **Smart text fitting** that shrinks a text layer until it fits its box, plus a Text Studio, brush and adjustment tools.
- **Smart Suggestions** such as auto enhance, colorize and brighten.
- **Editor comforts:** mouse-wheel zoom, aspect-ratio lock, light and dark themes, undo and redo, and export of either the full canvas or just the raw photo.

## How it works

The editor is a React and TypeScript app built with Vite. Fabric.js drives the canvas and its history, `@imgly/background-removal` segments the image on the client, TensorFlow.js with COCO-SSD finds the subject for crop suggestions, and Colorthief extracts the palette. State lives in Zustand stores. The project was planned and coded with the assistance of IBM Bob.
