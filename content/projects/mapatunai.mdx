## The problem

Checking a public infrastructure project means piecing together the official record, the contractor behind it and what is actually on the ground. MapaTunAI puts all three on one map.

## What we built

- **A national map of DPWH infrastructure projects**, seeded with PSA regions and provinces and drawn with Mapbox GL.
- **Contractor records, citizen reports and satellite history** attached to each project.
- **An AI audit:** Gemini cross-checks project records, satellite images and citizen reports to flag irregularities.
- **A 26-endpoint API** that serves the map, the records and the audit.

## How it works

Project, contractor and report data lives in PostgreSQL on Supabase, accessed through Prisma. The Next.js app renders the map with Mapbox GL and calls Gemini to run the audit on a project's records, imagery and reports.
